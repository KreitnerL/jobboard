## jobboard v0.10.0

### New features

#### Cluster panel
- **What it is:** a third panel below Jobs and Resources, collapsed by default. It shows every Slurm node and this
  machine, grouped by partition, with how much of their CPU cores, RAM and GPUs is allocated and which jobs hold them.
- **How to use it:** expand **Cluster** in the Job Board. In the terminal: `jobrun cluster` (`-j` lists the jobs on
  each node, `-p PARTITION` shows only that partition, `--json` for scripts).
- Traffic lights per resource and per node: 🟢 up to 20% allocated, 🟠 up to 80%, 🔴 above. A node's light shows its
  fullest resource; the palette button switches to emptiest, GPU first, CPU only or RAM only (`jobboard.clusterIcon`).
  Nodes that take no new jobs (down, drained, not responding) are greyed out.
- Expanding a node shows a CPU, a RAM and a GPU row. Below each are the jobs that hold it, with owner, amount, time
  limit and latest end. A **Queued** row lists the jobs the scheduler plans to start on the node, with their expected
  start. Your own jobs are blue.
- Partitions with several nodes get a section each; nodes alone in their partition (typically workstations) share
  **Other nodes**, and this machine has its own. The tree button or `jobboard.clusterGroupBy` groups by every
  partition, by GPU model, or not at all.
- This machine counts the larger of what Slurm allocated and what it actually uses, so work started outside Slurm
  shows up too.
- Light on the cluster: one `sinfo` and one `squeue` call per refresh, a few milliseconds each, shared by all windows
  that use the same registry for 10 s. The panel refreshes every 30 s while it is expanded and visible;
  `jobboard.clusterSeconds: 0` refreshes only with its button.
- Without Slurm, or when the controller does not answer, the panel shows this machine and says what went wrong and
  what it needs: `sinfo` and `squeue` on PATH, able to reach the controller.

#### Expected start of queued Slurm jobs
- **What it is:** a Slurm job still in the queue shows when the scheduler expects to start it, on which node, and why
  it waits: `waiting for Slurm queue · start ~Fri 15:15 on node2 (Priority)`. `jobrun ls` shows the same.
- The estimate is Slurm's own and moves as other jobs end early or higher-priority jobs arrive. Jobs waiting for a
  dependency usually have none.

#### Filter in Resources and Cluster
- **What it is:** the funnel button in the panel's title bar. Type part of a node, job or user name, a GPU model or a
  partition, and only the matching rows remain, with the rows above them expanded.
- The filter stays across refreshes until you clear it with the × next to the funnel.

#### Resources or jobs below a node
- **What it is:** choose what expanding a node shows: CPU, RAM and GPU rows with the jobs below them (default), or
  the jobs directly, with the node's row showing CPU, RAM and GPU in columns.
- **How to use it:** the list button in the panel's title bar, or `jobboard.resourcesNodeDetails` and
  `jobboard.clusterNodeDetails` (each panel has its own).

### Changed
- Resources has a collapse-all button, and its rows line up in columns.
- In Resources, a node's row no longer shows a "free …" summary while CPU, RAM and GPU rows are below it; those rows
  carry the numbers.
- Long job names are shortened in the middle (`sweep_vit_large_…lr3e-4_seed2`); the tooltip has the full name.
