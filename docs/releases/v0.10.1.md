## jobboard v0.10.1

### Fixed
- `jobrun wait --any` could report a job's end twice: once in the wait that found it, and again in the next wait
  started with its `rearm:` line. This happened when the job ended while the wait was reading the registry. Each end
  and `JOBRUN_EVENT` is now reported exactly once across rearmed waits; at worst one `--interval` later.
- After a rearm, `jobrun wait --any` could miss a Slurm worker of a pool that ended shortly before the previous wait
  returned, because Slurm states are cached for up to 30 s. It is now reported.

### Changed
- Cluster panel, jobs view: a node's state (`this machine`, `⛔ drained*`) now comes right after its GPU column,
  before the job counts, so a narrow sidebar no longer cuts it off.
