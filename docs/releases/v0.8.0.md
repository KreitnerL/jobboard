## jobboard v0.8.0

### New features

#### Progress on its own row
- **What it is:** when you expand a running job, pool or group, its progress bar, rate, ETA and times move to a
  row of their own under the name. The sidebar can stay narrow and still show the progress.
- **How to use it:** nothing to do; collapsed rows keep everything on one line, as before. To always use one line,
  turn off the setting `jobboard.progressRow`.

### Changed
- A job that uses almost all of the resources it reserved no longer shows a warning icon. Using what you reserved
  is good, not a problem.

### Fixed
- A pool that no job joined (for example because its workers failed to start) stayed "waiting" forever and kept
  its group and `--after` jobs waiting too. It can now be stopped or removed from the sidebar or with `jobrun rm`,
  and it is cancelled on its own after 10 minutes.
